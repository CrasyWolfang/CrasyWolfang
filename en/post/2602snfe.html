Snow Fest

Event photography
published: TO COME

The following photos were taken for the student paper Lundagård.

It's a day in February. The cold temperatures have started to rise off and it has begun to rain lightly. Music was playing in front of Lund University. Many of the passersby stopped to take a photo or two. In front of them, Lund's Extreme Sports Association (Lunds extremsportarsällskap, LESS) had piled up snow from the local ice rinkref and built a makeshift ramp around with scaffolding.

Rail jam

The forenoon was quiet and a man was riding down the ramp by himself. This part of the event was the rail jam in which anyone could ride down the ramp. The staff informed the photographer that there were a set of skies available for rent and that he could try to go down the slope with them for free. Towards the afternoon the rain stopped and both the number of skiers and spectators grew. At first, most skiers would ride down the ramp and do simple jumps, but as the crowd increased, the skiers got bolder and attempted backflips and other stunts. It is a warm up for the upcoming competition after all. A ski competition in the middle of the city is rare, and the news outlets had caught an ear onto the event. A photographer from the regional newspaper, Sydsvenskan, handed one of the skiers a GoPro in an attempt to capture some footage. Meanwhile, a videographer from the national news channel interviewed people.

Competition

The clock turned three and it was time for the competition. The skiers lined up in a queue in front of the ramp, and even though it was a competition, the mood was high and people were cheerful, even as the audience held their breath as some skiers attempted some violent flips. Finally, towards the end of the competition, staff put up a fence for the competitors to jump over.
